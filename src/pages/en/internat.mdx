# Boarding

A boarding house is available to CPES Paris-Saclay students. The request is made directly when you enrol at the CPES, after your admission on Parcoursup, through a form on Démarche Simplifiée. Places are allocated as applications are processed, following a social-priority approach that benefits scholarship students first. For late enrolments, boarding applications are handled at the end of August, subject to availability.

The boarding house is located at the Lycée International de Palaiseau and offers single or shared rooms, common areas, a laundry room and a kitchen. It also stays open on weekends.

You can also check the housing website of the Université Paris-Saclay: there are many student residences on the Saclay plateau, as well as many CROUS housing options.

For meals, you can turn to CROUS facilities. The school canteen is also available, including in the evening for CPES students, through a package or included directly in the cost if you stay at the boarding house. Several small shops and snack spots can also be found nearby.

Feel free to reach out to [our students](/en/nos-etudiants) to find out more about boarding or the various housing options.
