# Welcome to the future CPES29 students

You have been admitted to the first year of CPES Paris-Saclay for the 2026 intake and will soon join the CPES29 cohort. This page brings together the essentials to prepare for your arrival: your holiday assignments, the prospective L1 group and the student union guide.

## Holiday assignments

The teaching team has shared preparatory documents (instructions, mathematics workbook, economics bibliography and readings) to consolidate your knowledge before the start of the year. You can find them in the [CPES29 holiday assignments](/en/documents-officiels#devoirs-cpes29) section of the official documents.

## The prospective L1 group

Are you a prospective student of CPES Paris-Saclay for the 2026 intake, a future CPES29? The student union has created an official group for prospective L1 students. Fill in the <a href="https://tally.so/r/XxQgqz" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">registration form</a> to be added. Proof of admission may be requested after you submit this form, before you are added to the group.

## The student union guide

The student union (BDE) has written a guide for newly admitted students: campus life, study organisation, useful tips and advice to make the most of your integration. You can find it on the <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DZS04DGAgGF/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">BDE's Instagram post</a>.
