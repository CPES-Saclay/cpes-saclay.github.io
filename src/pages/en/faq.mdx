# Frequently asked questions

<FaqQuestion question="What is the Paris-Saclay CPES?">
The CPES (Cycle Pluridisciplinaire d'Études Supérieures) Paris-Saclay is a selective three-year programme, the fruit of a collaboration between ENS Paris-Saclay, HEC Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris, the Lycée International de Palaiseau and Université Paris-Saclay. It leads to a multidisciplinary bachelor's degree combining mathematics, computer science, data science, economics, and social sciences or health.
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="How long is the programme and what degree does it award?">
The programme lasts three years, totalling 180 ECTS distributed across Bachelor 1, 2 and 3. It awards an institutional degree conferring bachelor's degree status, recognised by the State and jointly awarded by Université Paris-Saclay, Institut Polytechnique de Paris and HEC Paris.
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="Where are classes held?">
Courses are held primarily at the Lycée International de Palaiseau Paris-Saclay (LIPPS), located on the Paris-Saclay campus. The other courses are taught by the partner institutions: ENS Paris-Saclay, HEC Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris and Université Paris-Saclay. Depending on the course, students go to the partner's campus, or the partner's teachers come to the lycée; some courses alternate between the two. The typical weeks for [year 1](/en/programme/l1#semaine-type), [year 2](/en/programme/l2#semaine-type) and [year 3](/en/programme/l3#semaine-type) show which institution teaches each course.
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="Is there a specialisation track?">
Yes. From the first year, students choose between a **Health track** and a **Society track**, each representing approximately 22 ECTS of specialised courses spread over the three years.
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="What are the application and tuition fees?">
There are **no application fees**: admission is exclusively through Parcoursup. Tuition fees are the **standard university fees** (national registration fees and CVEC).
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="Is it possible to work while studying?">
The programme's pace is **very demanding** (lectures, projects, personal work), making it very difficult to hold a job alongside the curriculum. Students are strongly encouraged to explore scholarships and financial aid. To get an idea of the pace, see a [typical week in year 1](/en/programme/l1#semaine-type).
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="Can the CPES be combined with other programmes?">
No. The CPES is a full programme in its own right that replaces the first year after the baccalaureate. Its intensive pace is incompatible with dual enrolment.
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="What do students aim for at the end of the programme?">
Not everyone aims for the same thing. The multidisciplinary curriculum keeps several doors open, and each student builds their own plan: some aim for a university master's in applied mathematics, data science or AI, others for an engineering or business grande école, an école normale supérieure, a master's abroad or a path towards research. From the second year, master's presentations organised by the partner institutions help everyone refine their choice.
</FaqQuestion>

<FaqQuestion question="Where do graduates go next?">
Graduates go on to selective master's programmes, engineering schools, business schools, écoles normales supérieures or research, in France and abroad. The first cohort's destinations are detailed on the [career opportunities](/en/debouches) page, and [some graduates describe their path](/en/temoignages).
</FaqQuestion>
